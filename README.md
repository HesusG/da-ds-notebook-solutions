Welcome, this repository aims to be the starting point for both new members of the team and a guide for older members.

[CONTEXT] The team is made up of Reviewers, Tutors and Instructors, these 3 pillars must work in synergy with camaraderie and solidarity so that we can ensure a good experience for students.

[OBJECTIVE] The objective of this repository is one, to have the same information base between the teams. In this way, both reviewers and "tutors and instructors" can understand the guidelines of the work of each of the parties and thus ensure that we all have a holistic view of what is reviewed and taught to students.

[IMPORTANT] In this area of ​​the Bootcamp for Data Science and Data Analyst we look for a professional behavior, this includes being aware that we are working for an international company where there are different positions and responsibilities, that we must communicate with respect, that we can all contribute value and that the best way to do a good job is by working and trusting as a team.

[REPOSITORY STRUCTURE] This repository aims to be simple and concise, you will be able to find each project, its description, its template, its solution, its data, relevant information and the reviewer criteria as well as the teaching dynamics of tutors and instructors.

It is important to understand that the templates are in jupyter notebooks since it is the dynamics that students face in each sprint and the most common practice that a junior Data Scientist/Analyst could face.
